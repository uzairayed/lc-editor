# Viewpoint

Triggers: overlook, mountain, snow field, forest edge, valley from above, a named peak in frame.

## Intent

A viewpoint is a **vista**. The viewer needs to stand there. This is the Murree-class hold: wide land, then one closer if you have it.

## Hold

- First wide of the land: **vista**.
- Second angle of the same valley: **breath** or drop.
- If this is the destination (the reel is the view), **vista** can sit at 5.0 to 5.5s and you may skip a **reveal** elsewhere.

## Role

`site_wide`. `hook` when the land is the striking first frame. `closer` on a last quiet wide.

## Cut

Enter on the widest readable land. Exit before a pan that repeats the same ridge. Hard cut to a **detail** (snow texture, a sign, hands) or to leaving.

## Caption

Place and one number if you have it ("Murree, 7,000 ft"). Adjectives last.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` across the ridge, slow. Punch belongs on a foreground object, not the sky.

## Done

The land has been stood in, and you have not stacked three wides of the same valley.

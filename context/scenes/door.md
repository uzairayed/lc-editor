# Door

Triggers: closed door, open door, doorway, hand on a handle, someone pushing or pulling a door, walking through a door.

## Intent

A door in travel footage is a **reveal**. The camera is about to show the room, courtyard, or tomb. The viewer needs time to enter with it. A door flashed at **ack** reads as a leftover, not an arrival.

## Hold

- We open it, we go through it, or the interior starts behind it: **reveal** (5.0 to 8.0s). Take 10.0s only when the source is one continuous take and the reel is already in the upper half of 15 to 28s.
- We ride or walk past a door that stays closed and we never enter: **breath**.
- One **reveal** door per reel. A second door in the same cut is **breath** or it is dropped.

Analysis may split a long take at 8.0s. If both keyframes are this door action, add them as one clip.

## Role

`site_wide` when the door opens onto the destination. `journey` when we pass it. `hook` only when the door *is* the famous thing (a named gate belongs on `gate.md`).

## Cut

Enter on the hand hitting the handle, or the first frame the door is readable as a door. Exit after the interior is established, or on the frame the body clears the threshold. Hard cut. If the next clip is the room, leave a sliver of interior on this clip so the join feels walked, not jumped.

## Caption

The picture is the sentence. If you caption, name the place ("Makli gate"), not the action.

## Motion

Video: `none`. A still of a door: `kenburns`, slow, **ack** to **breath**. `punch` only when a carving or knocker on the door is the point (then also read `carving.md`; **detail** wins if you punch).

## Done

A stranger understands we are entering, and the reel still has room for the interior that follows (at least one **detail** or **breath** of what is behind the door).

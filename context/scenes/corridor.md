# Corridor

Triggers: hallway, corridor, arcade you walk down, colonnade as a tunnel.

## Intent

A corridor is a guided walk toward a payoff. Keep it long enough to feel the tunnel, short enough that the payoff still owns the reel.

## Hold

- Walk toward a lit end or an opening door: **breath** to **vista** (3.0 to 4.5s). If the walk *ends* in a door, the door card owns the hold; this clip stays **breath**.
- Static empty hallway with no walk: **ack**, or drop it.

## Role

`journey` between rooms. `site_wide` when the arcade *is* the site (long tomb corridor).

## Cut

Enter already moving. Exit as the end of the corridor opens, a frame before the next space is fully wide. Hard cut.

## Caption

Silent, or the place name if this is the first readable architecture.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` down the vanishing point.

## Done

The walk has a destination on the next clip. The corridor is not the closer.

# Sign

Triggers: place-name board, milestone, street sign, brown tourism sign, painted mileage.

## Intent

A sign is a caption the world already wrote. Hold it long enough to read, then leave. It can replace a text caption. Two destination boards that are a list, not a walk: read `pair.md` and stack them.

## Hold

**detail** if the letters are the point (stay until a short name is readable). **breath** if the sign is in a wider passing shot.

## Role

`journey` on the road. `site_wide` when the sign is the site's name at the gate. `hook` only if the board itself is the joke or the payoff.

## Cut

Enter when the text is sharp. Exit as soon as the name has been readable. Crop or punch rather than holding a tiny board in a wide.

## Caption

Skip the text layer when the sign already says the line. If you caption, add the fact the sign does not (distance, time), not a repeat of the words.

## Motion

`punch` onto the letters, or `none` if already tight. Still: `kenburns` into the board.

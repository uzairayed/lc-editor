# Night

Triggers: lamps, neon, lit streets, shrine lights after dark, vehicle lights on a road.

## Intent

Night is a different place. Exposure and captions are harder. One lit street or shrine is enough to say "after dark."

## Hold

**vista** for a readable lit street or shrine. **breath** for a pass of lights on the road. Neon signs: **detail** if the letters are the point (`sign.md` if they name the place).

## Role

`site_wide` for a lit place. `journey` for road lights. `closer` is strong. `hook` when the lights are the striking first frame.

## Caption

Keep type off busy neon unless you can park it in a dark patch of the 22 to 50% safe zone. Prefer a calmer frame over a style change.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` along the lights. Punch on a single lamp or sign, not the whole street.

## Done

Night is established once, captions still read, and you have not cut three similar streets of lights.

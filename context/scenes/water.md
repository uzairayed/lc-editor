# Water

Triggers: lake, river, fountain, well, canal, a pour of water that is the place (not chai: that is `food.md`).

## Intent

Water gives motion and sound. A fountain or lake is a place; a well can be a **detail**. Natural ambience stays if the owner chose natural audio.

## Hold

- Lake, river, or a courtyard fountain as the wide: **vista**.
- Well, tap, or a tight pour of water: **detail**.
- Water in the background of a walk: **breath**.

## Role

`site_wide` for the body of water. `site_detail` for well and fountain sculpture. `closer` is strong on still water.

## Cut

Enter when the surface is readable. Exit after light on the water has landed. Hard cut. A match from wheel-spin to a swirl is legal.

## Caption

The name of the water if it has one. Silent if the picture is the lake.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` along the shore. Punch on a fountain head, not on empty surface.

## Done

The water has one job (place or detail), and the next clip is a different scale.

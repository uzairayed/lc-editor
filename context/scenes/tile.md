# Tile

Triggers: tilework, mosaic, turquoise glaze, kashi, glazed brick, repeating geometric tile.

## Intent

Tile is why people save the reel. It needs sharpness and time to read the repeat. This is the Karachi-series "slow the cutting down" beat.

## Hold

- Hero tile wall or floor: **detail** to the low end of **vista** (2.8 to 4.0s). Prefer the long end when color is the story.
- A glance of tile while walking: **breath**.
- Two tile clips: the second must be a different scale (wall then a single motif), each **detail**.

## Role

`site_detail`. `hook` only if the tile is more striking than any wide. Grade: protect turquoise and sandstone (`instructions.md` when the Karachi preset is on).

## Cut

Enter on the sharpest, most even plane. Exit before the camera hunts for a new patch of the same wall.

## Caption

Silent. Material names ("Kashan blue") only if the owner asked for that voice.

## Motion

Video: `none`, or a slight `punch` as the motif lands. Still: `kenburns` across the repeat, slow.

## Done

The viewer remembers a color and a pattern, and you have not cut three similar tile walls back to back.

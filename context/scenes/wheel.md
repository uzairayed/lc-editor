# Wheel

Triggers: tire, spokes, wheel-spin, a rolling wheel filling the frame.

## Intent

A wheel is match-cut material: spin to dome, rubber to curve. Alone it is texture. Paired it is craft.

## Hold

**ack** to **breath**. Keep it short so the match has room. If you never pair it, treat it as `texture.md` and consider dropping it.

## Role

`journey`. The cut *into* a `dome.md` or `tile.md` clip is the reason to keep it.

## Cut

Enter on a clean spin. Exit on the strongest curve, into the matching site frame. Hard cut. This is a match cut, not a dissolve.

## Caption

Silent.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` with the rotation, **ack**.

## Done

The wheel has a partner shot, or it is off the timeline.

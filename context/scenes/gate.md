# Gate

Triggers: gate, portal, entrance arch you walk or ride through, checkpoint, shrine doorway at site scale.

## Intent

A gate is the site announcing itself. It is larger than a room door and often the first readable architecture on arrival.

## Hold

- We approach and pass through, or the camera holds on the gate as the destination: **reveal** if it is the arrival, **vista** if we stay outside and read the whole gate.
- Gate in the background of a ride-by: **breath**.
- One arrival-gate per reel.

## Role

`site_wide` on arrival. `hook` when the gate is the most striking first frame of the destination (better than a highway). `journey` on a ride-through that is not the site.

## Cut

Enter on the first frame the gate fills enough of the frame to read as a gate. Exit once we are through, or after the inscription or tile on the gate has been readable for a beat. Hard cut into the courtyard or facade.

## Caption

Place name, or nothing. "Thatta gate" beats "arriving at the entrance."

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` pulling toward the opening. `punch` on an inscription only (then `carving.md`).

## Done

The viewer knows we have arrived at a named place, and the next clip is inside or on the facade, not another road.

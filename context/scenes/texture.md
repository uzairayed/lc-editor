# Texture

Triggers: stone grain, wood, fabric, rust, cracked plaster, close material with no readable object.

## Intent

Texture is glue between wides. It is also the first thing to cut when the reel runs long. Keep it only when it matches something (stone to facade, rust to gate).

## Hold

**detail** (2.4 to 2.8s). If you cannot name what the texture belongs to, drop the clip.

## Role

`site_detail`. Never `hook`. Never the only site coverage.

## Cut

Enter on the sharpest grain. Exit after the material has registered. Match-cut into the wide it belongs to, or out of that wide.

## Caption

Silent.

## Motion

`punch` or `none`. Still: `kenburns` across the grain, **ack**.

## Done

The material has a partner building or object, or it is off the timeline.

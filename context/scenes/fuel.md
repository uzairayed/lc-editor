# Fuel

Triggers: fuel pump, petrol stop, Cafe Imran, Gharo, any planned food or fuel stop on the route.

## Intent

A stop is a named beat in the journey, not more road. It gets one clip and one caption.

## Hold

**breath** to the low end of **vista** (2.8 to 4.0s) so the place is readable. A pour or plate at the same stop is `food.md` on a *second* clip, **detail**, only if the food is the story of the stop.

## Role

`journey`. In the Karachi preset this is the food-stop caption slot.

## Cut

Enter on the readable sign, pump, or seating. Exit before idle wandering. Hard cut back to road or on to the site.

## Caption

One concrete line: "Cafe Imran, Gharo" or "one fuel stop". This is the journey's second caption (distance/time is the first).

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still of the cafe front: `kenburns`.

## Done

The stop has a name on screen or is visually unmistakable, and you have not spent two wides on the same canopy.

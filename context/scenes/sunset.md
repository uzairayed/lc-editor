# Sunset

Triggers: golden hour, dusk sky, sun on the horizon, warm sidelight on a facade.

## Intent

Sunset is a closer and a grade cue. It is expensive: the same sky twice is a slideshow.

## Hold

**vista** if the sky or the lit facade is the shot. **breath** if sunset is only a wash on a clip you kept for another subject (then that subject's card owns the hold).

## Role

`closer` first. `hook` if the reel opens on the best dusk frame of the destination. `site_wide` when the dusk facade is the establish.

## Cut

Enter on the strongest color. Exit before the sun is a tiny dot in empty sky. Hard cut, or the 4-frame luma fade only if this is the last frame (`instructions.md` closer hold).

## Caption

Silent, or the invite line if this is the closer ("Full route in Ep 2").

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` along the horizon, slow. End hold of 0.3s on the last frame when this is the closer.

## Done

Dusk appears once, and if it is the closer the invite line has had its caption hold.

# Facade

Triggers: building wide, tomb wide, the whole site from outside, elevation, front wall.

## Intent

The facade is the postcard. It proves we are at a place. One strong wide beats three similar wides. Two named buildings that are a list, not two angles of one site: read `pair.md` and stack them.

## Hold

- First establishing wide of the destination: **vista**.
- Extra angles of the same building: **breath** or drop.
- If a gate or courtyard already did the establish, this clip is **breath**.

## Role

`site_wide`. `hook` when it is the most striking first frame (prefer this over a highway). `closer` on a last quiet wide.

## Cut

Enter when the building is readable as one object. Exit before a slow wander along the same wall. Hard cut to **detail**.

## Caption

The payoff line belongs here if it has not been said ("600-year-old city of tombs").

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` across the elevation. No punch on the whole building.

## Done

The viewer can name the building from memory of this frame, and a closer shot follows.

# Stairs

Triggers: stairs, steps, climbing, descending, a stairwell.

## Intent

Stairs are motion you can cut on, and they also show scale. A climb into a tomb or a descent into a court is part of arriving.

## Hold

- Climb or descent that delivers us to the site: **breath** to **vista** (3.2 to 4.5s) so the change of level registers.
- A few steps in a corridor with no destination change: **ack** to **breath**.
- Hands on a rail plus feet: one clip, **breath**. Split only if you need a `hands.md` **detail** later.

## Role

`journey` on a walk between places. `site_wide` when the stairs *are* the architecture (wide tomb steps, ghat). `hook` only if the stair itself is the striking first image.

## Cut

Enter mid-stride, not on a planted foot. Exit on a landing or as the upper/lower space opens. Hard cut on the step, matching the next clip's motion direction if you can.

## Caption

Silent. If you name a level ("up to the dome"), one short line.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` along the rise. No punch on empty steps.

## Done

The viewer feels the level change, and the next clip is the space the stairs opened, not more stairs.

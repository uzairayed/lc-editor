# Food

Triggers: plate, pour, stall, steam, chai, karahi, hands serving, a bite landing.

## Intent

Food is a sensory hold. Steam, pour, and the first readable dish are the shot. Talking heads at a table are not. Two plates or two cups of one kind that are a list: read `pair.md` and stack them.

## Hold

- Pour, steam, or the hero plate: **detail** to **breath** (2.6 to 3.5s). The pour needs the whole action if the source has it.
- Stall wide (the place, not the dish): **breath**, then a **detail** of the plate if you keep both.
- One food beat per reel unless the reel *is* the stop.

## Role

`journey` on a route stop. `site_detail` when the place is a food site. Mute clips where bystanders talk (`instructions.md` / owner sound policy).

## Cut

Enter on steam, the pour start, or the plated dish. Exit at the end of the pour or when the plate is readable. Hard cut.

## Caption

The dish or the stop, one line. "Beef biryani, Gharo" beats "delicious local food."

## Motion

Video: `none`. `punch` as the pour hits the cup or the plate lands. Still: `kenburns` across the dish.

## Done

The viewer can name the food or the stall, and you have not cut three similar plates.

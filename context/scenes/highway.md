# Highway

Triggers: road, highway, ride-by, overtake, traffic, N-5, open tarmac.

## Intent

The road proves the journey. It is punctuation, not the destination. A reel that opens on tarmac wastes the hook.

## Hold

- One journey proof (lean, overtake, landmark passing): **breath**.
- A second road clip: **ack** to **breath**, only if it shows a new fact (a named landmark, a change of weather).
- Highway as the first clip: replace it with a site **hook** if you have one.

## Role

`journey`. Never `hook` when a site frame exists. `closer` only as a leaving shot after the site, and only **breath**.

## Cut

Cut on motion: into a corner, an overtake, a landmark sliding out. Enter already at speed. Exit mid-motion into the next journey clip or the site.

## Caption

Distance, time, or route. Numbers. One line for the whole journey block, not one line per road clip.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still of a road: `kenburns` along the vanishing point, **ack**.

## Done

The viewer knows we traveled, and the longest section of the reel is still the site.

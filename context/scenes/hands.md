# Hands

Triggers: hands making, pointing, touching stone, pouring, holding a ticket, a map, a cup.

## Intent

Hands stand in for a face. They show work and scale. On the Karachi preset, this is the human beat that replaces a selfie.

## Hold

**detail**. If the hands complete an action (pour, push a door, take a print), hold the whole action even if that nudges toward **breath**.

## Role

`site_detail` at the site. `journey` for a pour or ticket on the road. Never the only site coverage.

## Cut

Enter when the hands are already in frame. Exit at the end of the gesture. Match-cut is strong: hand on tile to full tile wall.

## Caption

Silent. The object they touch can take the caption on the next clip.

## Motion

`punch` at the moment of contact, or `none`. Still: `kenburns` toward the fingertips.

## Done

One gesture has completed, and the next clip shows what the hands were for (door, tile, food).

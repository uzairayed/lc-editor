# Carving

Triggers: relief, stone carving, inscription, plaque on a wall, door knocker, carved screen.

## Intent

Carving is information at arm's length. The viewer is meant to lean in. Hold long enough to read a motif or a word, then leave.

## Hold

- Readable motif or inscription: **detail** (2.4 to 3.0s). Stretch toward 3.0s if letters are in frame.
- Carving as texture while we pass: **ack** to **breath**.
- If the carving is on a door you are already holding as **reveal**, stay on the door clip; do not add a second carving clip unless the motif is a different idea.

## Role

`site_detail`. `punch` is at home here.

## Cut

Enter on the sharpest plane of the motif. Exit when the shape has been readable for a beat. If letters are in frame, stay until a short word could be read.

## Caption

Transcribe only a short proper name already in the stone. Otherwise silent.

## Motion

`punch` on the motif, or `none`. Still: `kenburns` into the relief.

## Done

One motif has been seen clearly. The next clip is a different scale (wide or hands), not another inch of the same wall.

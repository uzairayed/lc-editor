# Dome

Triggers: dome, cupola, tomb roof, onion or drum seen against sky.

## Intent

A dome is a silhouette and a shape. It is match-cut material (wheel to curve) and a closer candidate.

## Hold

- Dome as the site's signature against sky: **vista**.
- Dome as a passing roof while we walk: **breath**.
- Tight drum, tile on the dome, or finial: **detail** (also read `tile.md` if glaze is the point).

## Role

`site_wide` for the silhouette. `site_detail` for drum and tile. `closer` is strong here. `hook` when the dome is the striking first image.

## Cut

Enter when the curve reads against sky or courtyard. Exit after the shape has landed. For a match cut, leave on the cleanest curve.

## Caption

Silent, or the site name if this is the first identifiable frame.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` up the curve. `punch` only on a finial or inscription.

## Done

The curve has been used once as a picture, and if you match-cut, the other side of the cut is ready (`wheel.md`).

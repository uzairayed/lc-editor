# Helmet

Triggers: helmet, visor down, rider POV, mirrors, gloved hands on the bar from the cockpit.

## Intent

The cockpit is the rider's body in the frame without a selfie. Visor-down is the allowed human on the Karachi preset.

## Hold

**breath**. One cockpit clip per journey block. A second is **ack** and only if it shows a new fact (rain on the visor: also read `weather.md`).

## Role

`journey`. `hook` only if the visor reflection *is* the striking first image (reflection of the site is a match-cut into tile or dome).

## Cut

Enter on a stable visor or mirror frame. Exit as the reflection or the road ahead becomes the next picture. Hard cut, or match the reflection to the site.

## Caption

Journey numbers can sit here if the road is too busy to read type.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still of a visor: `kenburns` into the reflection.

## Done

The rider is present once, and the site still owns more time than the cockpit.

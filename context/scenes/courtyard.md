# Courtyard

Triggers: courtyard, open court, inner yard, cloister green, tomb enclosure from inside.

## Intent

This is often the site's true wide. After a door or gate, the courtyard is where the viewer stands.

## Hold

- First time we see the open court: **vista**.
- A second angle of the same court: **breath**, or drop it.
- If you already spent a **reveal** on the door into this court, keep the courtyard at the low end of **vista** (3.5 to 4.0s).

## Role

`site_wide`. `hook` when the court is the single most striking first frame of the destination. `closer` if you end on a quiet hold of the same court.

## Cut

Enter on the widest readable frame. Exit before a wander that repeats the same walls. Hard cut to a **detail** (tile, carving) rather than another wide.

## Caption

Place name if it has not landed yet. One line.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns`, slow, across the court. Punch belongs on the next detail, not here.

## Done

The viewer can draw the site's footprint, and the next clip is closer (tile, carving, dome), not another wide of the same yard.

# Weather

Triggers: rain, fog, dust, mist, spray on a visor, a dust plume on the road.

## Intent

Weather is atmosphere and a reason to keep natural audio. One weather clip grades the whole reel; two is a mood reel.

## Hold

**breath** to **vista** when weather *is* the picture (fog on the mountain, rain on the court). **ack** when it is only texture on a ride you already kept.

Rain on a visor: one clip, **breath**, and `helmet.md` for cut and role.

## Role

Whatever role the underlying place has. Weather does not invent a new section. `hook` when fog or rain is the striking first frame.

## Cut

Enter when the weather is readable (streaks, haze, particles). Exit before a long hold of empty haze. Hard cut to a sharper site frame.

## Caption

One concrete word plus the place ("fog on the pass") if the picture could be missed as a grade.

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns`. Outdoor denoise stays on for ride wind (`SPEC-CRAFT-08`).

## Done

Weather has been felt once, and the site or journey is still identifiable under it.

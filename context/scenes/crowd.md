# Crowd

Triggers: vendor, queue, prayer group, bystanders, a busy street, a group at a shrine.

## Intent

People give scale and life. Speech on the bed is a problem: mute those clips unless the owner asked to keep voices.

## Hold

**breath** for a readable group. **ack** if faces dominate and you only need proof of life. A prayer or ritual that *is* the site: **vista**.

## Role

`site_wide` when the gathering is the place. `journey` for a market passed through. Karachi preset: no face selfies; a crowd wide with no hero face is fine.

## Cut

Enter on the widest readable group. Exit before a hunt for faces. Hard cut to architecture or hands.

## Caption

The activity or the place ("Friday at the shrine"), not "lots of people."

## Motion

Video: `none`. Still: `kenburns` across the group, keep faces small.

## Done

Scale is clear, speech is muted unless the owner kept it, and the reel returns to place or journey.

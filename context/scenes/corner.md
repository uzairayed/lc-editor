# Corner

Triggers: lean, turn, hairpin, bike or car rolling through a bend.

## Intent

A corner is the cleanest motion cut you will get on a ride. Use it as a join, not as a scenery hold.

## Hold

**breath**. If the corner reveals a vista (lake, mountain, site), the *next* clip owns **vista**; this clip stays **breath**.

## Role

`journey`. Good into a site **reveal** or a `viewpoint.md` clip.

## Cut

Enter already in the lean. Exit at the apex or as the new view opens. Hard cut. A whip into the site is earned here (`specs` transitions: 2 to 3 per reel).

## Caption

Silent. Journey numbers belong on a calmer road clip.

## Motion

Video: `none`.

## Done

The turn has joined two places. You have not stacked three corners.

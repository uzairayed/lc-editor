# Title

Triggers: creator names, style names, or job types that should open this file. Keep them on INDEX too.

## When to use

The project type or owner request that calls for this style.

## Intent

Why this compile style exists. What the viewer expects from this creator's format.

## Compile

Ordered beats. A numbered list of sections the video walks through.

## Hold

Which hold words from `context/HOW.md` apply to which beats. Note any beat that upgrades to **reveal** or **vista**.

## Role

Roles from `context/HOW.md`: `hook`, `journey`, `site_wide`, `site_detail`, `closer`.
Process roles: `before`, `wash`, `detail`, `after`, `hero`, `skip_face`, `engine`, `wheel`, `interior`, `machine`.

## Shot keep/skip

Which shots to look for. Which to skip.

## Cut

The frame you enter on, the frame you leave on. Any section-change transitions.

## Caption

When the picture is enough. Caption style for this creator (card, stroke-and-shadow, burned-in dialogue).

## Motion

`none`, `kenburns`, `punch`, `zoom_in`, `zoom_out`, or `zoom_pair`, and when.

## LC mapping

## Done

What a stranger understands after watching a video in this style.

## Unverified
